The aim of this experiment is to study the angular distribution of the gamma raditaion from the $\gamma-\gamma$ cascade from the decay of $^{60}$Co to $^{60}$Ni, by setting up a Fast-Slow Coincidence circuit. After first calibrating the components of the circuit setup, we set one detector to rotate in a $180\degree$ range around the $^{60}$Co source, while a second detector sat stationary at the same distance from the source as the first outside the semicircle traced out by the first detector. With this setup we took first a measurement of the random coincidences, followed by a measurement of the true coincidences.\\
